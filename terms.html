Transport services

Summit provides ground transportation and logistics for organs and tissue, medical equipment and supplies, laboratory specimens, and clinical and surgical teams, on both emergency and non-emergency runs.

Summit is a transportation provider. We do not provide medical care, clinical judgment, patient treatment, or determinations about the suitability, viability or handling requirements of any cargo. Those determinations remain with the client and its clinical staff.

Requests and confirmation

  • A transport is not booked until Summit confirms it and issues a trip number.
  • Requested times are targets. We plan and drive to meet them, but we do not guarantee arrival times, and we are not liable for delays caused by traffic, weather, road closures, mechanical failure, acts of government, or conditions at the origin or destination.
  • Emergency response is provided in accordance with applicable state and local law and only where Summit is authorized to operate in that mode.

Your responsibilities

  • Package, label, seal and temperature-condition cargo correctly before hand-off, and tell us any special handling requirement in advance.
  • Provide accurate origin, destination, contact and timing information.
  • Have authorized personnel available to release cargo at pickup and receive it at delivery.
  • Do not tender hazardous materials, controlled substances or regulated shipments without disclosing them and confirming in writing that we can carry them.
