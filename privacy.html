What we collect

Summit Medical Transport Inc. ("Summit," "we," "us") collects information you give us and a limited amount of technical information collected automatically when you use summitmedicaltransport.org.

Information you give us

  • Contact details you submit through our inquiry form — name, organization, email address and phone number.
  • The content of messages, quote requests and job applications you send us.
  • Transport request details provided by phone or, once it launches, through the client portal: origin, destination, timing, cargo type and billing contacts.
  • Account credentials and billing information if your facility opens a client portal account.

Information collected automatically

  • Standard server log data: IP address, browser type, referring page, and pages viewed.
  • Cookie and analytics data as described under Cookies.

How we use it

  • To respond to inquiries and provide quotes.
  • To dispatch, perform, document and bill for transport services.
  • To send transport status updates by text or email when you request them.
  • To evaluate job applications.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of our systems.
  • To meet legal, regulatory, accreditation and recordkeeping obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Health information

In the course of transport work we may handle protected health information (PHI) on behalf of hospitals, organ procurement organizations, laboratories and other covered entities. That information is governed by our agreements with those organizations — including any business associate agreement — and by applicable law, not by this policy.

This website is not a place to send PHI. Please do not include patient identifiers, donor identifiers or clinical details in the inquiry form. Use the dispatch line for anything case-specific.

Who we share it with

  • Service providers who operate on our behalf — hosting, dispatch and vehicle-location software, email and messaging, and payment processing — under contracts limiting their use of the data.
  • Clients and their authorized staff, for the trips they have requested, including custody documentation and status updates.
  • Legal and regulatory recipients where required by law, subpoena, or to protect the safety of people or property.
  • A successor in the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

Cookies

This site uses only the cookies required for it to function. If we add analytics or advertising tools later, this section and any required consent banner will be updated before those tools go live. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings.

Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide services and to meet our legal, contractual, tax and recordkeeping obligations, then delete or de-identify it. Transport records tied to a trip number are retained under the schedule required by our client agreements and applicable law.

Your choices

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you and request a copy.
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong.
  • Ask us to delete information we are not required to keep.
  • Opt out of transport status messages by replying STOP or contacting us.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy law. Email privacy@summitmedicaltransport.org and we will respond within the period the law allows.

Security

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information we handle, including encrypted transmission, access controls and staff training. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee that transmissions to this site are immune from interception.

Children

This site is directed at healthcare organizations and their staff. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the "last updated" date above, and for material changes we will provide notice through the site or directly to account holders.
