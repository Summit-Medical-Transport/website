This policy describes what our website and apps do today. It has not yet been reviewed by counsel and may be revised after that review.

Scope

This policy explains how Summit Medical Transport, Inc., a Colorado corporation ("Summit," "we," "us") handles personal information collected through:

  • this website, summitmedicaltransport.org;
  • the Summit web app and the Summit mobile app for iOS and Android, which clients use to request and follow transports and which our dispatchers and drivers use to run them (together, the "apps"); and
  • the transport services we provide, including calls to dispatch.

If you are a client organization, your contract with us may contain additional terms about the information we handle for you. Where it does, the contract controls.

What we collect

When you visit this website

This website has no contact forms, no sign-up forms, no analytics and no advertising tools, and it does not set cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which records standard request information (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) in its server logs. The site's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser also sends a request, including your IP address and browser information, to Google.

When you use the apps

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, organization and role, and the account number we assign. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash.
  • Transport requests: pickup and drop-off locations and facility details (building, floor, room, suite), airport and flight details if a stop is at an airport, on-site contact names and phone numbers, a point of contact for the trip, special instructions, times, priority, and what is being moved (for example equipment details, organ type, or blood product types and unit counts), plus notes added by you or our staff.
  • Passenger details, only if a request includes passengers: name, date of birth, phone number, email address, driver's license number if provided, personal belongings, food preferences and allergies, special needs and medical notes.
  • Chain-of-custody records for blood and specimen shipments: cooler, seal and unit identifiers (such as ISBT 128 donation identification numbers), temperature readings (taken by hand or uploaded from a cooler's data logger), inspection results, exceptions such as temperature excursions and how each was resolved, and the name, organization, role and handwritten signature of the person who releases or receives each shipment. Signatures are also captured at drop-off on other trips.
  • Trip records: status changes, and any change made to a request after it was submitted, with the original value, the new value, who made the change and why. Crew also record when they turn emergency lights and sirens on and off, who authorized it and the reason.
  • Messages sent through in-app chat, including a location if you choose to share one.
  • Driver location: while a driver or pilot is on an assigned trip with the mobile app open, the app sends the device's location to our dispatchers in real time. The app does not collect location in the background. While the trip is in progress, we also store the trip's route: about one point every 15 seconds, or sooner when the vehicle has moved 50 meters, with the position, its accuracy, speed, heading and time. Recording stops when the trip ends. We also store arrival and departure times at each stop, and the device's position when the driver checks in at a stop or captures a hand-off signature, with its distance from the stop.
  • Camera: the mobile app uses the camera only to read barcodes on coolers, seals and blood units. The barcode value is kept; the camera image is not saved or sent to us.
  • Activity records: to keep an audit trail, we record who created, viewed, changed or downloaded records and when, along with sign-in and sign-out events and failed sign-in attempts, and the IP address and browser or device type involved.

When you call us

Dispatchers record the transport details you give them in the apps. We do not currently record phone calls.

How we use it

  • To accept, plan, dispatch, route and perform transports.
  • To document chain of custody and give the shipper, receiver and requesting client a record of each shipment, including downloadable custody reports (PDF and CSV).
  • To show dispatchers where vehicles are during active trips, and to keep a record of the route each trip took as part of its trip and custody record.
  • To create and manage accounts, including sending account emails such as a welcome message with sign-in instructions.
  • To protect the apps and our clients' information: authentication, access control, fraud and abuse prevention, and audit trails.
  • To meet legal, regulatory, contractual and recordkeeping obligations.

We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for targeted advertising, and we do not use it to build marketing profiles. The booking form lets you ask for status updates by text or email; we do not yet send automated trip notifications, so dispatch will contact you directly.

Health information

Some transports involve health information, for example passenger medical notes, or specimens and blood units that a hospital or blood center can link to a patient or donor. We use that information only to perform and document the transport. Before we handle protected health information for a hospital, laboratory or other HIPAA-covered organization, we will sign a business associate agreement with that organization. That agreement will govern the information in addition to this policy.

Please do not send patient or donor details through this website or by email. Give case-specific information to dispatch or enter it in the apps.

Who we share it with

  • Your organization and the people on the trip. Customers in the same organization can see that organization's requests. Assigned drivers and pilots see what they need for the trip, including passenger and contact details. Custody records and trip records are available to the requesting client and others in its organization, the assigned crew and our dispatch staff, and the people who sign at pickup and drop-off see the shipment they sign for. Dispatch sees the full stored route of a trip. The client and crew see a summary of it (distance, times and a sample of points); for blood and specimen transports, the downloadable custody report also lists every stored route point.
  • Service providers that run parts of our service:
    • Amazon Web Services (United States): servers, database and account email (Amazon SES);
    • Vercel: hosts the web app's files and records standard request logs;
    • Expo: builds the mobile app and delivers app updates to devices (the app checks Expo's update service, which receives standard request information such as IP address and app version);
    • Google: address lookup, maps, routing and travel-time estimates (Google Maps Platform receives addresses and coordinates, and the maps shown in the apps are loaded from Google by your browser or device), and fonts on this website;
    • GitHub: hosts this website;
    • Apple and Google: distribute the mobile app through their app stores.
  • Legal and safety recipients when the law requires it, in response to valid legal process, or to protect the safety of people or property.
  • A successor if Summit is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets. The successor must continue to protect the information as this policy describes.

Cookies and device storage

Neither this website nor the apps use advertising or analytics cookies. To keep you signed in, the web app uses a secure, http-only cookie that holds your sign-in token for our server; your other sign-in details are kept only in memory while the page is open. The web app also keeps a few small, non-identifying values in your browser's local storage, such as whether a session may exist and the time of your last activity, which it uses for automatic sign-out. The mobile app keeps your sign-in tokens and basic profile in your device's secure storage (the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore), and your light or dark display preference in app storage. Signing out removes your sign-in details. You can also clear them through your browser or device settings.

Retention

We have not yet adopted a formal retention schedule. Until we do:

  • Transport requests, trip records, chain-of-custody records, stored trip routes and the activity audit trail are kept indefinitely. Custody records, trip routes, the lights-and-sirens log and the audit trail are designed so they cannot be edited or deleted, because clients and regulators rely on them.
  • When an account is deleted or closed, it is deactivated and no longer usable. Its name and contact details stay linked to the records that account created. See Deleting your account.
  • A driver's location outside an in-progress trip is relayed to dispatch and not stored.
  • Server and hosting logs are kept for limited periods set by our hosting configuration and providers.

We will update this section when we publish a retention schedule.

Security

The safeguards in place today include:

  • encrypted connections (HTTPS) between the apps and our servers;
  • passwords stored only as salted one-way hashes, with a minimum length;
  • short-lived sign-in tokens, sessions that end 12 hours after sign-in, sign-in rate limits and temporary lockout after repeated failed attempts;
  • automatic sign-out after 15 minutes without activity in the web and mobile apps (a driver or pilot with a trip in progress stays signed in until the trip ends or the 12-hour limit is reached, so dispatch keeps receiving their location), and in the mobile app, hiding the screen contents when the app is in the background;
  • sign-in tokens on mobile devices kept in the device's encrypted secure storage;
  • role-based access, so each person sees only the trips and records their role and organization allow;
  • an append-only, tamper-evident audit trail of changes and of who viewed records; and
  • signed custody hand-offs that are timestamped by our server and checked for changes.

We are still adding safeguards, including multi-factor sign-in, which the apps do not offer yet. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee that information will never be accessed without authorization. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as the law requires.

Your choices

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you and request a copy.
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong. Account holders can change their password in the apps; contact us to correct other details.
  • Delete your account, or ask us to delete information. We will delete what we are not required to keep. Custody records, signatures on them, trip routes and the audit trail must be kept for our clients' regulatory records, so we cannot delete those. See Deleting your account.
  • Turn off location or camera access for the mobile app in your device settings. Drivers need location during trips, and the camera is needed to scan barcodes, though you can type identifiers instead.

You may have additional rights under the privacy law of your state. Email privacy@summitmedicaltransport.org and we will respond within the time the law allows. If we handle your information for a client organization, such as a hospital, we may refer your request to that organization.

Deleting your account

You can delete your Summit account in either of two ways:

  • In the mobile app: open Profile, tap Delete Account and confirm.
  • By email, if you use the web app or can't sign in: write to privacy@summitmedicaltransport.org from the email address on your account, with the subject "Account deletion request" and your name and organization. We may contact you to confirm the request comes from you before we act on it.

When your account is deleted:

  • it is deactivated straight away: you are signed out on every device, the account can no longer sign in, and it is no longer offered as a contact in the apps;
  • your name, email address, phone number, organization and account number are kept, linked to the transport requests, messages, trip and custody records and audit trail entries the account was involved in, and your email address cannot be used to register a new account. If you change your mind, contact us and we can restore the account;
  • transport requests, chain-of-custody records (including signatures), trip records and routes, and the audit trail are kept, as described under Retention. Our clients rely on these records to show who handled each shipment and when, and custody records, routes and the audit trail are designed so they cannot be changed or deleted.

Deleting your account in the app does not by itself erase that information. If you also want your personal information deleted, say so in your email. We will delete or de-identify the personal information we are not required to keep, tell you what we deleted, and tell you what we kept and why. Copies of your details inside custody records and the audit trail cannot be removed.

Children

This website and the apps are for healthcare organizations, their staff and our own staff. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account or use the apps. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through them. A minor's information may appear in a transport request a client makes for a passenger, and it is handled like any other passenger information. If you believe a child has given us information directly, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

When we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. If the change is material, we will also notify account holders through the apps or by email before it takes effect.
